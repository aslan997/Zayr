# Zayr — Lore & Canon

Canon story, characters, world, and terminology. This is the source of truth
for narrative content. **Never silently overwrite established canon** — if an
implementation detail appears to conflict with this document, stop and flag
the conflict rather than resolving it unilaterally.

> The story is a fictional fantasy interpretation inspired by the concept of
> Jinn. It is not intended as a literal theological explanation of Islam and
> makes no claims about actual Islamic theology.

## 1. The Jinn

The Jinn ruled the world before humanity existed. Their civilization became
increasingly powerful and, over time, increasingly arrogant and corrupt.

Jinn architecture and technology are described in [GDD.md](GDD.md) §2 —
enormous, geometric, vertically integrated, alien but beautiful, built around
manipulation of Veyr. Explicitly **not** based on real-world Middle Eastern,
Arabian, desert-kingdom, or European medieval aesthetics.

## 2. Veyr

A supernatural energy/essence the Jinn manipulate directly, serving
simultaneously as:
- energy
- supernatural essence
- technology medium
- weapon medium
- environmental force

Skilled Jinn do not use manufactured weapons (swords, bows, axes) — they
manipulate Veyr directly. Jinn bodies, technology, architecture, and weapons
are all connected to Veyr. See [COMBAT.md](COMBAT.md) for the Veyr Edge,
Zayr's manifested Veyr weapon.

## 3. The Fall

A conflict occurred between the Jinn and heavenly forces, driven by the
civilization's growing arrogance and corruption. The Jinn were defeated and
their civilization collapsed. Some Jinn survived but became shadows of what
they once were. Humanity would not arrive until thousands of years later —
the game takes place long before humanity's rise.

## 4. Zayr

Zayr is one of the surviving Jinn. He has lost much of his memory. The game
gradually reveals, through exploration and memory-gain (see the core loop in
[GDD.md](GDD.md) §3):
- the civilization
- Zayr's relationships
- the fall
- Zayr's role in the events
- what happened to his friends
- why the civilization was lost

None of these specifics are defined yet beyond what's stated here — they will
be added incrementally as story milestones are reached. Do not invent
specifics ahead of that.

## 5. Major Characters

Canon names (do not rename): **Rhaek, Seyra, Vael, Nayra, Auren.**

Their exact characterization, relationship to Zayr, and boss mechanics are
**not yet specified**. Treat as reserved names only until further design is
provided.

## 6. World Regions

One interconnected civilization — regions should read as different parts of
the same whole, not unrelated fantasy biomes:

1. **Avaris** — Fallen Capital
2. **Ember Depths**
3. **Sky Citadel**
4. **Drowned City**
5. **Celestial Threshold**
6. **Final Memory / Ending**

No further detail on these regions exists yet.
